Two things in live preview that looked wrong rather than worked wrong.

## A `---` rule no longer shows its dashes

A horizontal rule is meant to read as a divider, with the dashes that produce it
hidden until you put the caret on the line. Since 1.10.0 the dashes stayed visible,
sitting on top of the rule in the punctuation colour.

The rule was hiding them by painting them transparent, and 1.10.0's syntax colours gave
a rule's dashes a colour of their own — which wins. They are now hidden outright, the
way a heading's `#` is, so no colour can bring them back. Put the caret on the line and
the dashes return, as every other piece of markup does, and the document itself was
never anything but `---`.

## The fold arrow is a drawn chevron

The collapse control was the text character `▾`, which arrives from whichever font
happens to carry it: a small dark blob, sat slightly off from the heading beside it, and
with the browser's own tooltip landing on top of the heading whenever you hovered it.

It is now drawn rather than typed — a chevron that turns to point at a collapsed section
instead of being swapped for a different character, aligned to the heading's first line
at any size, lined up in one column whether the heading is an `#` or a `######`, with a
proper hover target and no tooltip in the way.
